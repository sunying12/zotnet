🧠 Personality Guide

MBTI Types
DISC
Identification Tips

D — Dominant

Direct, decisive, results-focused. Driven by challenges and power. Motivate with goals and autonomy.

I — Influential

Enthusiastic, optimistic, collaborative. Driven by recognition. Engage with fun and social connection.

S — Steady

Patient, sincere, loyal. Driven by stability and cooperation. Engage with warmth and consistency.

C — Conscientious

Analytical, precise, systematic. Give them data and time to process. Respect their standards.

How to Identify Personality Types

👁 Observe first: Quick/intuitive decisions or slow/analytical?
🗣 Listening style: Do they interrupt, listen patiently, or ask clarifying questions?
💼 Work style: Structure (J/C/S) or flexibility (P/I)?
🔋 Energy: Gain energy from people (E) or solitude (I)?
📊 DISC in meetings: D = talks results · I = tells stories · S = checks on others · C = asks for data.
❓ Ask open questions: "How do you approach a new project?" reveals type quickly.
